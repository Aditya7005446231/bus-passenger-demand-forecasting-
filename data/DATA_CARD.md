# Data Card - Synthetic Bus Trip Demand Dataset

## Purpose
Practice and benchmark passenger-demand forecasting for an Indian intercity
bus-booking company: predict `passengers` per trip **7 days before departure**
to support capacity and scheduling decisions.

## Synthetic nature
All rows are generated by `generate_data.py` (seed 42). Nothing comes from a real
company. Patterns are plausible but invented; do not draw business conclusions from them.

## Files
- `bus_trips.csv` - main file (one row per trip).
- `ground_truth_events.csv` - **analysis only**: `disruption_flag`, `event_type`,
  `event_multiplier`, `uncapped_demand`, `clean_passengers`. Not available 7 days ahead.

## Column roles
| Role | Columns |
|---|---|
| Identifier (not a feature) | `trip_id` |
| Features (known 7 days ahead) | `route_id`, `origin`, `destination`, `distance_km`, `route_type`, `departure_datetime`, `weekday`, `month`, `season`, `is_holiday`, `is_weekend`, `is_peak`, `capacity`, `base_fare`, `days_to_departure` |
| **Target** | `passengers` |
| Derived from target (NEVER a feature) | `occupancy` = passengers / capacity |

## Period and size
2024-11-01 to 2026-10-31, daily, about 20,000 rows, 10 routes.

## Assumptions (demand model)
`demand = route_base x peak x weekday(route_type) x season(route_type) x holiday x trend x event x noise`
- Peak windows: 6:00-9:00, 16:00-20:00; peak multiplier drawn per route in (1.5, 2.0), off-peak = 1.
- Weekday effect depends on route type (commuter high Mon/Fri, tourist high Sat/Sun,
  overnight high Fri/Sun): {'commuter': [1.25, 1.0, 0.95, 1.0, 1.25, 0.8, 0.75], 'tourist': [0.8, 0.75, 0.8, 0.85, 1.1, 1.4, 1.35], 'overnight': [0.95, 0.9, 0.9, 0.95, 1.2, 1.1, 1.2]}
- Season effect by route type (tourist +/-25-30%): {'commuter': {'winter': 1.0, 'summer': 0.95, 'monsoon': 0.9, 'autumn': 1.05}, 'tourist': {'winter': 1.25, 'summer': 1.3, 'monsoon': 0.75, 'autumn': 1.0}, 'overnight': {'winter': 1.1, 'summer': 1.15, 'monsoon': 0.85, 'autumn': 1.0}}
- Holidays/festivals: multiplier drawn per holiday in (1.3, 1.6).
- Trend: yearly growth drawn per route in (0.05, 0.1).
- Noise: lognormal, sigma 0.1, mean 1.
- Disruptions: about 3% of days; 1-3 routes each; demand multiplier in
  (0.3, 0.6). Types: strike, heavy_rain, road_closure.
- Final passengers = min(round(demand), capacity), never negative.
- Capacity: fixed per route, swapped to another size with probability 0.05.
- Fare: distance x per-km rate by route type, +10% at peak; independent of demand.

## Injected data-quality issues (reproducible with the same seed)
- Missing values: 2% of `distance_km` and of `base_fare`, completely at random.
- Outliers: 0.75% of rows in `passengers` (typing errors: value x10 or entered as 0;
  x10 values can exceed capacity). `occupancy` is computed after this step, so it inherits them.
- Duplicates: 0.2% of rows copied exactly (same `trip_id`).

## Known limitations
- Holiday dates are approximate and the same for every route and state.
- Seasons are month-based steps, not smooth curves.
- No price elasticity, competitor, weather-forecast or promotion effects.
- Disruptions are unpredictable by design, which puts a floor under achievable error.
- Capacity swaps are assumed known 7 days ahead (bus assignment is fixed that early).
- Missingness is random; real missingness is usually patterned.
- The effect sizes were chosen by hand, not estimated from real data.
